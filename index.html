07 FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Read our Privacy & Data Policy or contact the Mindwave AI team.

Do I need any design experience?

Not at all. AI Home Decore was created for everyday people. Just take a photo and choose a style — the AI handles composition, color harmony and furniture placement for you.

What kind of photo works best?

A well-lit, horizontal photo taken from a corner of the room at chest height. Try to include the floor, at least two walls and any windows. Avoid heavy blur or very dark images.

Will the design match my real room?

AI Home Decore keeps the structure of your room — walls, windows, doors and perspective — and restyles everything within it. Results are visual concepts meant to inspire; always measure before purchasing furniture.

Can I try different styles on the same room?

Yes. Generate as many variations as you like, switch styles instantly and compare them side by side to discover what suits you best.

Is AI Home Decore free?

AI Home Decore is free to download and includes free designs to get started. Optional premium features may be offered inside the app for higher-resolution exports and unlimited generations.

What happens to my photos?

Your photos are used to generate your designs and are handled according to our Privacy & Data Policy. You stay in control of what you save and share.
